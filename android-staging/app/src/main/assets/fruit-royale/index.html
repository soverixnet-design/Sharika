Fruit Royale

Choose a chip and tap a fruit. The chip is submitted immediately. The countdown closes betting; settlement can arrive later.

Fruit +1 and +2 display additional result fruits. ×2 and ×3 display the reward multiplier. Jackpot displays a separately supplied award.

Fruit Royale V2 uses real Sharika virtual coins. Lemon, Strawberry and Kiwi pay ×4; Mango and Apple ×6; Watermelon ×10; Plum ×15; Cherry ×20. The multiplier includes the original stake.

Round bonus odds: none 92%, Fruit +1 3%, Fruit +2 1%, ×2 2%, ×3 1%, Jackpot 1%. Fruit +3 is disabled. An independent draw selects the primary winning fruit; jackpot awards come only from the separate pool funded with 5% of each accepted bet.

In live mode your server controls balances, bet acceptance, timing, results, prizes, history and rankings. This interface does not authorize a payment.
