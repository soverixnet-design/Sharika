# Sharika Royal Gift Design Draft

These are prototype assets, **not live catalog items**. The new Golden Dragon
King is a 3D-styled animated cutout, not a fully rigged 3D character video.

The video gift requirement is: visually high quality, one overlay player,
sound in the gift file, no black background, and a lower-bandwidth fallback.
Real-device Android WebM alpha and audio playback must be verified before
turning on a catalog item. The MP4 is only a conventional non-transparent
preview and must not be used as an overlay.

Suggested high-tier **gift** prices (not applied):

| Golden Lion King | 2,000,000 |
| Royal Wedding | 5,000,000 |
| Golden Dragon King | 10,000,000 |

**Catalog category rule**: Role/achievement **Medal** is separate from
**Noble/SVIP** status and its tier-specific ornaments, frames and entries.
Do not label a Noble/SVIP package as a medal, and do not migrate existing
Noble entitlements into the medal inventory.
