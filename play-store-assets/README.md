# Sharika Google Play listing assets

Prepared for the Google Play Console listing of `com.soverixnet.sharika`.

- `sharika-icon-512.jpg`: 512 × 512 launcher-brand icon, derived from the existing `android-staging/app/src/main/res/mipmap-nodpi/sharika_logo.jpg` without changing the logo artwork.
- `sharika-feature-1024x500.jpg`: 1024 × 500 Royal Premium feature graphic, using the original Sharika mascot and wordmark as the design reference.

The Play Console short and full English descriptions were saved as a **draft** on 7 November 2026. The privacy policy URL was saved separately.

The store listing is not complete: real app screenshots (at least two 16:9 or 9:16 phone images) still need to be captured from the app, inspected and uploaded. Do not use generated fictional UI as screenshots.
